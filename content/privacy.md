This is a personal blog. It's built to respect your privacy by default.

**The short version:**
- No accounts, no sign-ups, no newsletters.
- No analytics, no tracking scripts, no advertising.
- No cookies are set by this site.
- I don't sell, rent, or share anything about you.

**What is actually involved:**

**Your theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice locally so the site opens the way you left it. That stays on your device and is never sent to me.

**Server logs.** Like almost every website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) automatically records standard technical data — your IP address, the page requested, and your browser type — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This is standard infrastructure logging, not tracking.

**Links to other sites.** If you follow a link to GitHub, YouTube, or anywhere else, you're on that platform and subject to its privacy policy, not this one.

**Your rights.** Since I don't hold a database of visitors, there's usually nothing to retrieve or delete. But if you email me and want that message erased, just ask.

If the site ever adds analytics or comments in the future, this page will be updated first.
