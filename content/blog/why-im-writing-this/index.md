## How did it all start

**I quit YouTube.** Cold turkey.

When you strip away your main source of media, you find out pretty quickly how much empty space is left in your head. So, like anyone trying to figure out if they're losing their mind, I started looking around online to see how other people handled it. I wanted to read about people who actually stepped away from the algorithm.

That's when I stumbled onto an old school personal blog, something started way back around 2015.
 In this day and age, reading a quiet, independent blog felt like stepping into a different dimension. 
 Just a person writing down their thoughts for whoever cared to look.

I've always wanted to document my passing obsessions about random stuff and learning about them, but whenever I thought about doing it on YouTube, the friction was massive. YouTube demands a job, a persona, a chase for metrics, money, and fame. You start worrying about algorithms and pacing instead of what you actually want to say. And tbh watching YouTube is a hell of a lot easier than making it, so the ambition always died before it started.


## Building the Stack

Around the same time, I was working through web development fundamentals HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Looking at how simple and minimal it was, I figured I could build something like it later once I was comfortable with HTML, CSS, and JS. It'd be a good test of my skills.

Til looked at the bottom of that blog I found and saw how it was made. I realized: Wait.. I could build this myself, right now !

I started looking into the tools they used, only to find the original setup was old, deprecated, and abandoned for six years. But digging deeper led me to a newer variant called [Tabi](https://welpo.github.io/tabi/). That worked perfectly. I realized I could spin up my own digital notebook in my free time while studying.

Of course, "spinning it up" turned into a two day rabbit hole of tweaking config files, and tuning Tabi until it felt right. If you like how this place looks and want to skip the headache, you can grab my exact setup from [my GitHub repo](https://github.com/iiBMT/blog).

### What This Place Is
There's no content strategy or hustle here just a personal journal.
A place to vent about problems, dump my thoughts, and talk about whatever is in my head.. including what it's actually like to pull the plug on a heavy YouTube addiction and try to rebuild a normal attention span.
If you're reading this, you're looking at someone figuring it out in real-time. 
**Welcome to the log.**
